ABOUT US

Our mission

Little Scientists Taipei began with Evelyn Lee and Alice Kuok, two Taipei American School students with a shared love of science and education.

Our purpose is simple: help elementary school children explore scientific ideas, practice English, and enjoy figuring things out together.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions & answers

Still curious about something?
We’d love to help

Who are the workshops for?+
Our workshops are designed for elementary school students. Check each event’s registration details for its specific age group and any participation requirements.
What language do you teach in?+
Sessions are conducted in English, with high school mentors guiding the activities. If you’re unsure whether a session is right for your child’s English level, contact us before registering.
What will my child explore?+
Children explore ideas from physics, chemistry, and biology through experiments and hands-on activities. The exact theme and activities depend on the workshop.
How do I find dates, prices, and locations?+
Visit our workshop page for current registration details. If no sessions are listed, contact the team to ask about upcoming opportunities. The venue and any fee are specific to each event.
Can you run a session at our school?+
Yes. School and organization sessions typically run for 1–1.5 hours and combine an explanation, an experiment, and a hands-on activity. We provide the materials, and an ordinary classroom is suitable. Get in touch to discuss your group.

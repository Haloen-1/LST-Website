ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Privacy & cookies.

This notice describes the features on this Little Scientists Taipei website. Last updated 24 September 2026.

Browsing this site

This website includes workshop signup forms. It does not include advertising trackers or visitor analytics added by Little Scientists Taipei. Visitors do not need to create an account to read the website. Photo hearts are decorative. Your choices are saved in this browser’s local storage, without a public count or a website account. Clearing browser storage removes them. This preference is not used for advertising or analytics.

The hosting service may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to serve and protect the website. If access requires signing in, that sign-in is handled by the hosting provider.

Language preference

The lst_language cookie remembers your English or Traditional Chinese choice for up to one year. You can change it using the language button or clear it in your browser settings.

Contacting us

Contact links open your Gmail session in a new tab with our address filled in as the recipient. Google may ask you to sign in or choose an account. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email. Please send only the information needed for your enquiry.

Event registration and other links

Each program may use a built-in signup form or an external registration provider. Built-in forms collect the participant name and parent/guardian email to manage the request. Requests are stored in the owner-only console and can be exported by the site owner. Submitting a request does not guarantee a place. A separate optional checkbox records consent to receive future workshop emails; registration does not require this opt-in. The site does not automatically send email. External providers may include Google Forms, SurveyMonkey, or Wix. Instagram, Facebook, and directions links open separate services. Embedded Google Maps load only after you click Show Google Map; Google may then process technical information and use its own cookies. Their own privacy notices and settings apply when you visit them. Review the event’s terms before providing registration information.

Google Form enquiries / Google 表單洽詢

The bilingual enquiry form collects your name, email, optional enquiry, and optional email-updates consent. Responses go to a Google Sheet controlled by Little Scientists Taipei. Only authorized team members have access. Submission does not confirm a workshop place. Google processes the embedded form under its privacy policy. Contact us to request correction, deletion, or removal from updates.

Signup protection

To reduce automated spam, signup attempts use a short-lived hashed identifier based on technical request information. Raw IP addresses are not stored in the signup database; the short-lived rate-limit records are cleared as new requests are processed.

For details about reactions and Google Maps cookies, see the cookie policy.

Website editing and photos

The site owner signs in to a protected editor. Draft text, links, and photos are stored online so the owner can review and publish updates. Uploaded photos become visible to visitors when included in the published website.

Questions about your information

For questions about an enquiry you sent to the team, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, email littlescientiststaipei@gmail.com.

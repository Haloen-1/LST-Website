03 / OUR STORY

Curiosity is better
when it’s shared.

Little Scientists Taipei began with Evelyn Lee and Alice Kuok, two Taipei American School students with a shared love of science and education.

Our purpose is simple: help elementary school children explore scientific ideas, practise English, and enjoy figuring things out together.

Cookie policy

Updated 25 September 2026.

Cookies used by this website

Photo hearts are decorative. Your choices are saved in this browser’s local storage, without a public count or a website account. Clearing browser storage removes them.

Sign-in and hosting

The hosting provider may use cookies for owner sign-in and service security. Visitors do not need to sign in to browse or request a workshop place.

Google Forms

The enquiry subpage embeds a Google Form. Loading it connects to Google, which may use its own cookies and process technical information under its policies. You can also open the form directly using the page’s link.

Language preference

The lst_language cookie remembers your English or Traditional Chinese choice for up to one year. You can change it using the language button or clear it in your browser settings.

Google Maps

Embedded Google Maps are loaded only when you click Show Google Map. Google may then use its own cookies and process technical information under its policies. Directions links open Google Maps separately.

Your choices

You can browse without reacting or loading a map. Use a heart button again to remove your reaction, and use your browser’s settings to manage cookies. This site does not include advertising or visitor analytics added by Little Scientists Taipei.

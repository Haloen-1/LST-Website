## Title

Little Scientists Taipei — Workshop enquiries & updates
台北小小科學家 — 活動洽詢與最新消息

## Description

Leave your name and email so our team can contact you about workshops. Submitting this form does not reserve or confirm a place. A parent or guardian should complete this form for a child. Your responses are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible to our team.

請留下姓名與電子郵件，方便團隊與您聯繫科學活動相關事宜。提交此表單不代表已預約或確認名額。兒童請由家長或監護人填寫。您的回覆將儲存於僅供團隊存取的私人 Google 試算表。

## Questions

1. Name / 姓名 — required short answer; parent or guardian name / 請填寫家長或監護人姓名。
2. Email address / 電子郵件 — required short answer with email validation; type your email / 請輸入您的電子郵件。
3. Workshop or enquiry / 感興趣的活動或洽詢事項 — optional paragraph.
4. Email updates / 活動消息 — optional checkbox, initially unchecked: “Please email me about future Little Scientists Taipei workshops. / 我願意透過電子郵件接收台北小小科學家的未來活動消息。”

## Confirmation message

Thank you! We received your information. Our team will contact you about your enquiry. This is not a confirmed workshop registration.

謝謝！我們已收到您的資料，團隊將與您聯繫。這不代表活動報名已確認。

## Intended setup

- Bilingual labels on the same form, English and Traditional Chinese.
- Responders enter their email; no Google sign-in requirement where account policy allows.
- Response summaries hidden from respondents.
- Link responses to a new private Google Sheet, with automatic rows for submissions.
- Publish the responder form and connect its actual URL to the website after creation.
- Do not email invitations or automatically subscribe respondents to another service.
- The optional email-updates answer determines which addresses may be used for workshop announcements.
